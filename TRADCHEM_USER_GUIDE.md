## 🔍 Search Usage Examples

### Effective Query Examples:
- ✅ "ginseng benefits"
- ✅ "turmeric anti-inflammatory"
- ✅ "ashwagandha stress"
- ✅ "ayurvedic medicine for energy"
- ✅ "traditional Chinese medicine herbs"
- ✅ "SMILES notation curcumin"

### Search Tips:
- ✅ Use specific ingredient names
- ✅ Include benefit keywords
- ✅ Mention traditional systems (Ayurveda, TCM)
- ✅ Ask about chemical compositions
- ✅ Request SMILES notations
- ❌ Avoid overly complex queries
- ❌ Don't use non-English text

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Start Simple**: Begin with basic queries like "ginseng" or "turmeric"
2. **Be Specific**: Ask about particular benefits or applications
3. **Explore Systems**: Try queries about different traditional medicine systems
4. **Chemical Focus**: Request SMILES notations or chemical compositions
5. **Learn More**: Use the chatbot to explore related plants and compounds
